Janus: Cellular Anomaly Detection

A dual-mask attention transformer for log-based anomaly detection in cloud-native cellular networks.
Cellular systemsAI/MLAnomaly detection
  • Single-Pass Dual-Mask (SPDM) attention captures procedural and temporal dependencies alongside semantic consistency in network-function logs.
  • Domain-aware modeling and training incorporate context from 5G procedures, logs, and relevant specifications.
  • Evaluated on cellular core logs to identify anomalies in complex 5G control-plane workflows.

Maestro

QoE-aware dynamic resource allocation in Wi-Fi networks using telemetry-driven QoE estimation and RL-based flow control.
Wi-Fi resource management QoE-focused Multi-agent RL
  • LSTM-based QoE estimator that relies solely on controller telemetry—no client modifications.
  • DDQN multi-agent policy that jointly selects access category and traffic priority per flow.
  • Evaluated on an enterprise Wi-Fi 6 testbed with up to 25× QoE gains over WMM and up to 69% over QFlow.

QoE-Aware Routing

Traffic-aware routing path selection to improve video-streaming and conferencing quality of experience.
Purdue + Juniper NetworksTraffic engineering
  • Characterizes streaming and conferencing traffic, including burstiness and the effect of background network load.
  • Selects paths using both average traffic load and its variation.
  • The paper's preliminary evaluation reports 14% average QoE improvement for streaming and 11% average packet-latency reduction for conferencing.

Coauthored with Juniper researcher Patrick Melampy, the 2023 paper acknowledges partial support from a Juniper Networks gift.

Stateless 5G Core

Examining the performance costs and optimizations of state management in cloud-native 5G core functions.
Distributed systemsCloud-native 5G
  • Studies procedural versus transactional statelessness and the latency cost of external state management and database operations.
  • Evaluates alternative state storage, sharing, and access strategies along network-function chains.
  • Follow-up work investigates ways to avoid redundant network-function state retrieval.
