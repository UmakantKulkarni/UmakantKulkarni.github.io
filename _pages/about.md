I am a Senior Staff Researcher in the Network Security organization at Palo Alto Networks, where I focus on network protocol decoding, traffic analysis, and evasion detection for Advanced Threat Prevention. My broader research interests span network security, distributed systems, and AI/ML, with an emphasis on secure, scalable, and high-performance networked systems.

Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Nokia Bell Labs, where I worked on distributed architectures for next-generation mobile core networks. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University, where I was advised by Prof. [Sonia Fahmy](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/fahmy/) at [PurNet Lab](https://gitlab.com/purnet-lab/purnet-lab.gitlab.io/-/wikis/home). My doctoral [dissertation](https://doi.org/10.25394/PGS.30752984) explored zero-trust security, network anomaly detection, and the performance and reliability of cloud-native cellular and Wi-Fi networks.

Before joining Purdue, I worked at Microsoft/Affirmed Networks on cloud-native mobile core software, network protocols, and distributed network functions. I also hold a master's degree in Computer Systems Networking from Northeastern University.

Research
======

My research lies at the intersection of network security, networked systems, and AI/ML. I am particularly interested in network protocol analysis, traffic inspection, and techniques for detecting evasive and anomalous behavior in complex network environments. My earlier work explored zero-trust security for cloud-native networks, transformer-based anomaly detection, distributed cellular core architectures, and learning-based resource management in Wi-Fi networks.
